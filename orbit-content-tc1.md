## 1. Topic & Angle
- **Trend/Topic**: Co-parenting routine consistency for ADHD/attention challenged children during household transitions.
- **Rationale**: Children with ADHD and executive functioning difficulties struggle with transitions and differing expectations between co-parenting households, which often leads to emotional dysregulation on handoff days.
- **Empathy Angle**: We aren't here to judge different parenting styles or demand identical households. Instead, we want to provide a portable, visual routine anchor (Mateo's schedule) that travels with the child to make transitions feel safe and predictable.
