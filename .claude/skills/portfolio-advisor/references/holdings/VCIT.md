## What it holds

Intermediate-term (5-10 year maturity) investment-grade US corporate bonds. Issuers include major US corporations across sectors: finance, utilities, industrials, healthcare. NOT government bonds — entirely corporate credit.

**Why hold VCIT at all:** Credit spread exposure (corporate bonds carry over Treasuries), duration positioning (6-year exposure), diversification from pure Treasury holdings. These justify a 10% allocation but NOT overweighting.

## Key risks

- **Duration risk:** ~6 years duration means ~6% price drop per 1% rise in rates
- **Credit risk:** Corporate bonds can widen in recessions; VCIT can lose value when equities fall (partial correlation)
- **Tax drag:** Most expensive income position per dollar of yield for NRA (after REIT)
