## What it holds

PDBC gains commodity exposure through **futures contracts**, not physical commodities. It holds futures on:
- Energy: WTI crude, Brent crude, natural gas, heating oil, gasoline (~45–50% of index)
- Metals: gold, silver, zinc, aluminum, copper
- Agriculture: soybeans, corn, sugar, wheat

Actual commodity holdings shift based on the "optimum yield" roll strategy (rolls into contracts with the best carry, minimizing roll cost vs. naive front-month rolling).

## Key risks

1. **Contango drag** — When futures markets are in contango (later contracts cost more than near-term), rolling forward loses value. The "optimum yield" strategy partially mitigates this but does not eliminate it. In sustained contango environments, PDBC can significantly underperform spot commodity prices.
2. **Energy concentration** — ~45–50% energy exposure. Portfolio is effectively a commodity-heavy energy bet in adverse energy markets.
3. **Active management risk** — Roll decisions are discretionary; manager may underperform the theoretical optimal roll.
4. **Cayman subsidiary risk** — Tax structure depends on PDBC maintaining C-corp status. If the fund restructures as a partnership, a K-1 will be issued and this tax treatment changes materially.
5. **Fee waiver expiry** — Current 0.59% expense ratio is waived; review August 2027 when waiver may expire and net expense ratio may increase.
