## What it holds

US Treasury Inflation-Protected Securities (TIPS) — government bonds where both the principal and coupon adjust with CPI inflation. When inflation rises, the principal increases and so does the coupon payment. Provides a direct inflation hedge with government credit quality.

## NRA tax treatment

| Income type | Rate | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| TIPS coupon interest | **0% withheld** | Portfolio interest exemption (IRC §871(h)) — TIPS are US Treasury obligations |

| Capital gains | **0%** | NRA exemption |
| Net real yield | **~2.5% + inflation** | Full yield retained — zero withholding drag |

**Best of both worlds:** Government credit quality, inflation protection, AND zero withholding. Among the most tax-efficient positions in the portfolio for an NRA after SGOV/SHY.

## SCHP in inflationary environments

SCHP outperforms nominal Treasury ETFs (like SHY) when realized inflation exceeds breakeven inflation rates. If the 7-year breakeven inflation rate is ~2.3% and realized inflation is ~4%, SCHP's real yield of ~2.5% effectively delivers ~6.5% total return vs SHY's nominal ~4.5%.

## Key risks

- **Real rate risk:** Rising real interest rates reduce SCHP's price (duration ~7 years)
- **Deflation scenario:** If CPI declines, principal adjusts downward (though TIPS protect against principal falling below face value at maturity)
