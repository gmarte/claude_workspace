# Orbit Weekly Content — 2026-05-21

## 1. Topic & Angle
- **Trend/Topic**: After-School Restraint Collapse (ASRC) in neurodivergent and ADHD children.
- **Rationale**: Children expend immense energy masking and regulating their behavior at school. Once home (their safe space), they collapse emotionally due to cognitive and sensory exhaustion.
- **Empathy Angle**: We want to validate that after-school meltdowns are not bad behavior or bad parenting—they are a sign of deep trust. We advocate for a "no-demand" decompression zone supported by physical snacks and quiet visual routines.
